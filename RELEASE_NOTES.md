Smart capture: let Budget read your payments for you.

- Share payment screenshots to Budget (several at once), or scan a paper receipt with the camera
- Budget can read payment notifications from the banking and e-wallet apps you choose
- Everything it finds waits in "To review" with the account, pocket and category already filled in from your history; check it and tap Add
- Itemized receipts can be split so each item goes to its own pocket
- ATM withdrawals become a transfer to Cash; the same payment seen twice is only added once

To start: Settings → Smart capture → paste a free Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com.
