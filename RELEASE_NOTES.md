Smart capture now works entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

- Receipts and screenshots are read by a text reader built into the app; it works offline
- Payment notifications, transfer screenshots and store receipts (struk) are understood with rules made for Indonesian banks, e-wallets and shops
- Categories are guessed from the merchant and items (Indomaret → Groceries, SPBU → Transport, Telkomsel → Phone & internet…)
- Corrections you make in To review are remembered for that merchant next time
- The Gemini API key is no longer used and has been deleted from your phone
